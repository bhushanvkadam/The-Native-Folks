About The Native Folks

The Native Folks was born from a simple belief — that food should be as pure as nature intended.

In a world where shortcuts have become the norm, we chose a different path. One that goes back to our roots — to traditional farming, honest practices, and ingredients that don't need fixing.

Our journey begins in the soil — enriched using natural methods like Jeevamrit, an age-old practice that nourishes the land without chemicals. The grapes are grown patiently, handpicked at the right time, and then sun-dried over days — not rushed, not processed.

What you get is not just a product, but a reflection of how food used to be.

No sulphur. No bleaching. No artificial additives.

Every batch is carefully tested to ensure purity, because we believe trust should never be assumed — it should be proven.

At The Native Folks, we're not just selling raisins.

We're bringing back a way of living — one that is raw, real, and deeply rooted in nature.
