Questions We Love Getting

Everything you want to know before your first bite.

What makes your raisins different from store-bought ones?
Most commercial raisins are sulphur-treated to achieve that golden colour and preserve shelf life. Ours are 100% sun-dried over 7 days with zero sulphur, zero bleaching, and no chemical treatment — which is why they're naturally dark and taste like real fruit. We also grow in Jeevamrit-enriched soil, which means richer flavour from the ground up.
Is any sugar added to these raisins?
No, there is no added sugar. The sweetness comes naturally from the grapes, and the product is lab-tested to ensure purity and no additives.
Why are these raisins darker than regular raisins?
Most commercially available raisins are treated with sulphur to make them look bright and uniform. Our raisins are naturally sun-dried without any chemical treatment, which is why they appear slightly darker — a sign of purity.
Are these raisins safe for daily consumption?
Yes, they are 100% natural, chemical-free, and safe for daily consumption, including for kids and families.
Do these raisins contain sulphur or preservatives?
No. These raisins are sulphur-free, preservative-free, and chemical-free.
How should I store these raisins?
Store in a cool, dry place and keep the pack sealed after opening. For best freshness, you can refrigerate after opening.
Where are these raisins sourced from?
Our raisins are sourced from farms in Jamakhandi, Karnataka, and processed using traditional sun-drying methods.
Why do these raisins taste different from market ones?
Since our raisins are not chemically treated or artificially sweetened, they have a more natural, balanced sweetness compared to regular market raisins.
